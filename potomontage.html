Photomontage:

Concept: For this photomontage, I explored the complex theme of mental health and isolation, designing the composition to evoke the aesthetic of a contemporary magazine cover. The central narrative focuses on the psychological toll of the pandemic era, using the distressed expression of my sister in a COVID mask as the emotional anchor. To symbolize the stinging, overwhelming nature of anxiety and intrusive thoughts, I incorporated surreal imagery of drifting jellyfish. The Statue of Liberty is integrated into the background to represent systemic isolation and the suffocating feeling of trapped freedom within society. To bring this vision to life without violating copyright laws, I used an original photograph that I took of my sister, while the supporting images of the jellyfish and the Statue of Liberty were sourced from copyright-free public domain archives.To achieve a seamless yet unsettling composition, I relied heavily on non-destructive editing techniques in Photoshop. I applied precise layer masks to isolate each element, allowing the organic shapes of the jellyfish to wrap around the main subject. To establish a cold, somber mood appropriate for a mental health theme, I applied a cool-toned photo filter directly to the main subject. Finally, I utilized the Overlay blend mode on both the jellyfish and the Statue of Liberty layers. This blending choice allowed the textures and colors of the background elements to merge dynamically with the focal point, creating a surreal, layered texture that enhances the overall psychological depth of the design
